## Project Proposal

### Marqui

Child-Friendly, Interactive Museum Experience with AI

### Introduction

Museums have a significant role in teaching children, but traditional formats of information can find it difficult to captivate children who are used to watching brief videos, using their cell phones, and applying interactive applications.

### Proposed Solution

We suggest an interactive learning game with a mobile app powered by artificial intelligence that transforms a museum visit into a learning game. Children scan an exhibit with a smartphone, and AI automatically recognizes it, then gives a short and interesting and easy-to-understand explanation about the exhibit.

Once children learn more about the exhibit, they can take quizzes and earn XP, which unlocks missions for them to visit other areas of the museum.

### Objective

The primary goal is to motivate children to be more engaging with the physical museum through technology, and not just by spending time with their phones. The app will handle a sequence of activities that will look something like this: Discover – Scan – Learn – Quiz – Explore – Unlock.

### Expected Outcome

The project will seek to improve children's interaction with museum displays, make learning more fun and give museums information on how engaged they are and what learning outcomes they are achieving.

### Key Technologies

* Artificial Intelligence
* Image Recognition
* Data Science & Analytics
* Gamification
* Mobile Application Development
